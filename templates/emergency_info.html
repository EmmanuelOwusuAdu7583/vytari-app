Low blood glucose (hypoglycemia)

Common signs: shakiness, sweating, confusion, rapid heartbeat, dizziness, blurred vision, irritability.

If conscious and able to swallow: follow your doctor's instructions for treating a low (often a fast-acting sugar source), then recheck glucose after 15 minutes.

Seek emergency care immediately if: the person loses consciousness, has a seizure, or cannot safely swallow.

warning

High blood glucose (hyperglycemia)

Common signs: excessive thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision, nausea, fruity-smelling breath.

Seek emergency care immediately if: there is vomiting, difficulty breathing, confusion, abdominal pain, or fruity breath odor — these can indicate diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), a medical emergency.
