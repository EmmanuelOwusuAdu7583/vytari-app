Vytari stores blood glucose readings, insulin logs, meal photos, and symptom reports you and your care team enter, solely to support your diabetes care coordination. Data is only visible to you and the doctor you are connected to. This is a closed-pilot deployment; accounts are created by an administrator and your doctor, not through public sign-up.
